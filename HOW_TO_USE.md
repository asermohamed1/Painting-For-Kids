# 🎨 How to Use Paint for Kids

Hello! This is your guide to drawing, colouring and playing games with shapes.
Every button has a picture. Find the picture, read what it does, and have fun!

> 💡 **Always look at the blue bar at the bottom of the window.**
> It tells you what to do next, like *"Click the second corner"*.

> 👪 **For grown-ups:** to start the program, see the [README](README.md).

---

## 🖼️ The window

- **Top:** the buttons (the toolbar)
- **Middle:** your drawing paper. All shapes go here.
- **Bottom:** the blue bar with messages for you

---

## ✏️ Drawing shapes

| Button | Name | How to use it |
|:---:|---|---|
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/TRI.jpg" width="44"> | **Triangle** | Click the button. Then click **3 places** on the paper, one for each corner. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/RECT.jpg" width="44"> | **Rectangle** | Click the button. Click **one corner**, then click the **opposite corner**. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/seq2.jpg" width="44"> | **Square** | Click the button. Click **once** where you want the **middle** of the square. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/hexx.jpg" width="44"> | **Hexagon** (6 sides) | Click the button. Click **once** where you want the **middle** of the hexagon. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/circle.jpg" width="44"> | **Circle** | Click the button. Click the **middle** of the circle, then click a point on its **edge**. Far away = big circle! |

> 🙂 If the blue bar says **"Invalid"**, don't worry! Just click in another place:
> - Squares, hexagons and circles need **enough room**. Click a bit further from the edges.
> - For triangles and rectangles, don't put two corners on the **same straight line**.

## 👆 Choosing a shape

| Button | Name | How to use it |
|:---:|---|---|
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/SELECT.jpg" width="44"> | **Select** | Click the button, then click **on a shape**. It turns **pink** so you know it's chosen. The blue bar tells you about it. Do the same again to un-choose it. |

## 💾 Saving and opening your drawing

| Button | Name | How to use it |
|:---:|---|---|
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/save.jpg" width="44"> | **Save** | Click it and **type a name** for your drawing (like `house`). You'll see it in the blue bar. Press **Enter**. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/upload.jpg" width="44"> | **Open** | Click it, **type the same name** you saved with, and press **Enter**. Your drawing comes back! (This replaces what is on the paper now.) |

> ⌨️ Typed a wrong letter? Press **Backspace**. Changed your mind? Press **Esc**.

## 🎮 Play mode: games!

First draw **at least 2 shapes**. Then click:

| Button | Name | What it does |
|:---:|---|---|
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/play.jpg" width="44"> | **Play** | Opens the games. The buttons at the top change. |

**The games:**

| Button | Game | How to play |
|:---:|---|---|
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/shape.jpg" width="44"> | **Find the shape** | The blue bar says something like **"PICK ALL CIRCLE SHAPES"**. Click every circle you can find! |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/colour.jpg" width="44"> | **Find the colour** | The blue bar says something like **"PICK ALL RED SHAPES"**. Click every red shape! *(Colour some shapes with the paint bucket first.)* |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/shapecolour.jpg" width="44"> | **Find the shape and colour** | The blue bar says something like **"PICK ALL BLUE SQUARE SHAPES"**. Click only the blue squares! *(Colour some shapes first.)* |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/restart.jpg" width="44"> | **New round** | Starts the game again with a new question. |
| <img src="Paint%20For%20Kids%20Project/images/MenuItems/draw.jpg" width="44"> | **Back to drawing** | Returns to the drawing buttons. All your shapes come back. |

**While you play:**
- ✅ Right shape: **"GOOD JOB"** and the shape hides.
- ❌ Wrong shape: **"FOCUS AND TRY AGAIN"**. That shape hides too, so look carefully!
- 🏆 Found them all: **"YOU ARE A CLEVER KID... YOU WIN"**. Then you'll see how many you got right and wrong.

## ⭐ Try this!

1. Click **Circle**, click in the middle of the paper, then click a bit to the side. 🔵
2. Click **Select**, then click your circle. It turns pink.
3. Click the **paint bucket**, then **yellow**. It's a sun! ☀️
4. Draw a **square** and a **triangle** on top to make a house. 🏠
5. Click **Save** and type `myhouse`, then press **Enter**.
6. Click **Play**, then **Find the shape**, and see if you can win! 🏆
